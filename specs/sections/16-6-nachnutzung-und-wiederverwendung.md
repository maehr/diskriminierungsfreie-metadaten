# Section Spec: 6. Nachnutzung und Wiederverwendung

source: `manuscript/sections/05-praxis-06-6-nachnutzung-und-wiederverwendung.qmd`

## Purpose

- Anticipate and guide reuse so that it does not reproduce discrimination or enable harmful interpretations.
- Provide concrete requirements for interoperability, provenance, licensing, machine access, and feedback loops.

## Required Structure

- `### 6.1 Interoperabilitaet erweitern`
- `### 6.2 Zitation und Provenienz`
- `### 6.3 Nutzungsbedingungen und Lizenzen`
- `### 6.4 Maschinenlesbarer Zugang`
- `### 6.5 Feedback und Korrekturschleifen`

## Required Elements

- Keep the framing that reuse is never neutral and creates new contexts.
- Keep guidance on: open formats + APIs/dumps; PIDs; versioned mappings/crosswalks.
- Keep the requirement for a citation guide and machine-readable citation metadata (e.g., `CITATION.cff`).
- Keep the CARE-oriented licensing and TK Labels mention.
- Keep the change policy/issue tracker and community co-curation feedback framing.
- Keep the checklist callout "Checkliste Nachnutzung und Wiederverwendung".

## Citation Expectations

- If adding new governance models or licensing frameworks, cite their primary documentation.
