# Section Specification: Related Work

## Purpose

Situate the paper in relation to prior work; identify a gap the paper addresses.

## Inputs

- `research/sources/reading-notes/*`

## Output Constraints

- Word count: 800-1600
- Must include: 2-4 clusters of literature, explicit differentiation
- Must not include: straw-manning
